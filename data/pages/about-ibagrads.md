# About IBAGRADS

IBAGRADS is a private test-preparation academy in Karachi, Pakistan. It is **not** IBA Karachi (the university); it prepares students for the admission tests of IBA and many other universities.

- Claims 20 years of experience, 20K+ success stories, 15K+ students placed in IBA, IOBM, SZABIST, NUST, GIKI and others, and 1,000+ testimonials.
- Why IBAGRADS (as stated by the academy): proven results, enthusiastic instructors, researched material, small class sizes.
- Current campaign: "Your 2027 Test Prep Starts Here". 2026 admissions test prep is in progress.

## Test-prep streams offered

| Stream | Prepares for |
|---|---|
| BCAT (Business Colleges Admission Test) | IBA, LUMS, IOBM, SZABIST, KSBL, CBM, NBS and other business schools |
| ECAT (Engineering) | NUST, PIEAS, FAST, GIKI, NED, COMSATS, UET, KU-UBIT, IIEE and others |
| MDCAT (Medical) | PMC MDCAT, NUMS, DUHS, JSMU, KMDC, AKU |
| International tests | SAT, GMAT, GRE |
| School | XI-XII (Pre-Engineering, Pre-Medical, Computer), IX-X (Science, General) |

Courses are offered as **classroom courses** (6 Karachi campuses) and **online courses**. See "Contact and campuses", "Upcoming courses" and "Online courses".

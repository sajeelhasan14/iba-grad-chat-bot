# Free counselling session

IBAGRADS offers a **free counselling session** to help students (and parents) plan test preparation: "Fast-track your child's learning goals & get better results."

- Available at all 6 campuses: North Nazimabad, Gulshan-e-Iqbal, Bahadurabad, Gulistan-e-Johar, DHA, Malir.
- No fixed dates or times are listed; the academy contacts you after you book.

## How to book

Fill the "Book your slot" form at https://ibagrads.com/free-session/ with:

1. Name
2. Phone / Mobile
3. Email
4. Current address
5. What advice you are looking for (short description)

Or call any campus directly (see "Contact and campuses").

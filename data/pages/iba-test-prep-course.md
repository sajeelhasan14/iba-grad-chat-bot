# IBA test-prep course (BCAT)

IBAGRADS prepares students for the IBA Karachi admission test through its **BCAT** course. The same material also prepares students for LUMS, CBM, SZABIST, KU and other business schools.

Not listed on this page: course duration, timings, fees, mock tests, or which campuses offer it. For batches and start dates see "Upcoming courses". For IBA's own fee structure and admission schedule, students should check the official IBA website (https://www.iba.edu.pk).

## Interview preparation

- IBA gives direct admission to undergraduate applicants with high test scores (cut-off defined year to year). Others must clear an interview.
- IBAGRADS gives an interview briefing on question types and formats.
- After the briefing, students get 3-4 mock interviews conducted by IBA graduates and students who faced the real IBA interview.

## Admission through SAT / GMAT / GRE (as stated by IBAGRADS)

Undergraduate (SAT I):

| IBA program | Math | Evidence-Based Reading & Writing | Total |
|---|---|---|---|
| BBA & BSAF | 600 / 800 | 600 / 800 | 1270 |
| BSSS & BS ECO | 600 / 800 | 600 / 800 | not stated |
| BSCS & BSEM | 640 / 800 | 600 / 800 | not stated |

Essay score is not required.

Graduate: GMAT 600, or GRE 160 Quant + 160 Verbal (320 total), gives test exemption.

## IBA eligibility criteria (as stated by IBAGRADS; confirm on iba.edu.pk)

Undergraduate:
- BBA: 2 B's and 1 C in three principal A-level subjects, or 65% in Intermediate.
- BS-ACF / SS / ECO / ECO-MATH / CS: 1 B and 2 C's in A-levels, or 60% in Intermediate.

Graduate:
- MBA (Morning/Evening) and MS: 2.5 CGPA or 60%, 16 years of education, 4-year degree from an HEC-recognized university. No work experience required.
- EMBA: 16 years of education and 3 years of work experience after education.

PhD (CS, Economics, Math, Statistics): MS / M.Phil or equivalent with 3.0 CGPA or 60%.

Post-Graduate Diplomas:
- Supply Chain, HR, Project Management: 14 years of education + 6 years of experience, or 16 years + 3 years.
- Healthcare Management: MBBS/BDS + 2 years hospital experience; nursing graduates + 5 years; other healthcare professionals with 16 years of education + 2 years hospital experience.

## IBA programs (as listed by IBAGRADS)

- Undergraduate: BBA, BS-ACF, BS-SS, BS-ECO, BS-ECO-MATH, BS-CS, BS-MATH
- Graduate: MBA (Morning/Evening), EMBA, MS-CS, MS Data Science, MS Development Studies, MS-ECO, MS-MATH, MS Islamic Banking and Finance, MS Journalism
- PhD: CS, Economics, Math
- Post-Graduate Diploma: Healthcare Management, Supply Chain Management, HR Management, Project Management, Public Policy
